FAQ

Will there be drinks and snacks?

Yes, drinks and snacks are provided free of charge.

Snacks usually include chocolate bars, cookies, and gummy candy. Drinks usually include water, Red Bull, and light beverages.

How many players can join and is there a waiting list?

A maximum of 9 players can play and there is no waiting list — first come, first served.

How can I sign up for a game?

Registration is handled exclusively through the WhatsApp group or direct contact with the organizer.

Is there a dealer?

Yes, there is a dedicated dealer.

Which card set is used?

Faded Spade FSX Modern cards are typically used.

Is Wi-Fi available?

Yes, free Wi-Fi is available.
